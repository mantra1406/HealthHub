About HealthHub

HealthHub is a modern digital healthcare platform designed to bridge the gap between patients and doctors. We believe healthcare should be simple, accessible, and paperless.

Our platform allows patients to book appointments, access medical records, view prescriptions, and manage their entire health journey — all from one place.

  • Trusted by 500+ patients
  • 6 specialist doctors onboard
  • Secure & private health records
  • Available 24/7 online
